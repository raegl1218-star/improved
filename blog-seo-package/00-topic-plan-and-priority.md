# Gloria Food SEO / GEO / AIO Blog Package

## Topic Research Context

Gloria Food should use blog content to support commercial pages, not replace them. The strongest B2B blog topics are the ones that answer procurement questions before a buyer sends an inquiry: how to evaluate an IQF supplier, how to prepare a private-label frozen vegetable project, and how to choose between product formats such as edamame and mukimame.

For Google SEO, the articles target supplier, certification, packaging and buyer checklist queries. For GEO and AI Overview style answers, each article includes concise answer blocks, practical checklists, comparison tables, FAQs, and procurement-specific language that an AI system can summarize accurately.

## 1. Topic Plan

| Priority | Blog Title | Target Keyword | Search Intent | Why It Fits the Website |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How to Choose a Reliable IQF Frozen Vegetables Supplier: A Buyer Checklist for Importers | iqf vegetables supplier | Commercial investigation / supplier evaluation | This topic captures importers, wholesalers and foodservice buyers who already need a supplier but are still checking risk, certificates, packaging and shipment control. It supports `/iqf-frozen-vegetables/`, `/food-safety-certifications/`, and `/factory-quality-control/`. |
| 2 | Private Label Frozen Vegetables for Supermarkets: Packaging, Quality and Launch Checklist | private label frozen vegetables supplier | Procurement planning / OEM decision | Supermarkets and food brands often search before sending product specs. This article can turn broad OEM interest into a structured inquiry by explaining packaging, MOQ, artwork, certificates, testing and launch preparation. |
| 3 | Frozen Edamame vs Mukimame: Product Differences, Applications and Buying Tips for Wholesale Buyers | frozen edamame supplier | Product comparison / purchase selection | Edamame and mukimame are high-intent Asian frozen food products. A comparison article helps buyers select the right format for retail bags, foodservice packs, sushi channels and ready-meal applications, then links to product inquiry pages. |

## 2. Publishing Priority Recommendation

1. Publish Article 1 first: it is the broadest procurement article and strengthens the main IQF frozen vegetables supplier topic.
2. Publish Article 2 second: it supports paid traffic and OEM/private label inquiries, especially for supermarkets and food brands.
3. Publish Article 3 third: it is more product-specific and can bring higher-quality inquiries from Asian food distributors, sushi suppliers and retail buyers.

## 5. Research Notes Used for E-E-A-T Alignment

- Google Search Central guidance emphasizes helpful, reliable, people-first content, so these articles avoid keyword stuffing and focus on buyer decisions, checklists and practical procurement details.
- CBI market-entry and buyer-requirement guidance for processed fruit and vegetables notes that many European buyers request third-party food safety certification and supplier documentation, so the articles discuss certificates as buyer approval tools rather than vague trust badges.
- FDA food allergy labeling guidance was considered for the edamame/mukimame article because edamame is a soybean product and buyer-facing labels may need soy allergen handling depending on destination market.
